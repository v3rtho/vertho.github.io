Welcome to my blog! My name is **Thomas Verheyden**, and I am a **Security Architect** based in Belgium.

After completing my bachelor's degree, I began my career in IT consultancy and worked my way up from support engineer to Security Architect. Over the past 16 years, I have worked with a variety of enterprise clients in different sectors, and for the last 9 years, I have been working as a freelance security consultant.

## What this blog is about

The purpose of this blog is to share my experience and knowledge with advising, designing, and implementing different security solutions, particularly those related to Microsoft. My blog will provide readers with tips and tricks, as well as insight into the knowledge I've gained from working with different security solutions. I believe that my blog posts should add extra value and not be a copy of the Microsoft docs.
